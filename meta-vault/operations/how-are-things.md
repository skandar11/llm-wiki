# How are things

> A [[query]] with a fixed question: the same rules — the catalogue first, every
> fact names its page, where the base is silent say so.

- **trigger:** phrase — "how are things", "what's on fire", "what haven't I
  finished"
- **executor:** agent
- **status:** possible

## Steps

1. Read `../../planning/plan.md`, `../../planning/backlog.md` and the
   `status.md` of every domain in the
   table of levels in `../../CLAUDE.md`.
2. Answer in chat: first blockers and deadlines (what is stuck and what gates
   it), then what is moving, then what the owner started and did not finish.
3. Where the base is silent — say so: an empty cell is not zero, the absence of
   a mark is not a debt.
