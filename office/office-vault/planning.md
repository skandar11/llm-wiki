# planning — the office's plan

The form is the top's — `../../meta-vault/planning.md`; here only what the
office keeps.

| Path | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `../../planning/plan.md` | **the one plan of the base** — the office's work in progress is a line there |
| `../planning/backlog.md` | tasks with no calendar nature, one line each (sort the inbox, bring a certificate): `essence — executor → pointer · status` |
| `../planning/backlog-closed.md` | closed lines with the date and the reason; born with the first |
| `../planning/tasks/<slug>/` | the run of a big task by the form [[tasks]] |

Anything with a deadline (filings, payments) — in [[tax-calendar]], not here.
