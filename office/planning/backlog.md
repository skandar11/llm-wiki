# Backlog — office tasks

Tasks with no calendar nature, one line per item: `essence — executor →
pointer · status` (sort the inbox, bring a certificate, update the profile).
Anything with a deadline (filings, payments) lives in [[tax-calendar]]. A task
bigger than a line → `tasks/<slug>/` beside it; closed lines leave for
`backlog-closed.md`. Rules — `office-vault/planning.md`.
