# tasks — the form of the cycle

A task that does not fit in a backlog line gets a records folder
`../planning/tasks/<slug>/`: `spec.md` (what and why — read by the owner
before the start) → `state.md` (the live state of the run) → `record.md` (the
retro: what came out, what we learned). The backlog line stays and points
here.

Here is only the form; the runs are records and live in `../planning/tasks/`.
